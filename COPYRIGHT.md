## Provenance

OpenCourant is based on the final public OpenRadioss commit:

    Upstream commit: 1a0d691b82bd4f94a3bc2399682551e84e1d5fa4
    OpenCourant tag: openradioss-final
    Snapshot date:   2026-09-28

The upstream repository (github.com/OpenRadioss/OpenRadioss) was removed
by Siemens on October 1, 2026, and no public archive was left. The
`openradioss-final` tag in this repository is the record of where the
upstream work ends and OpenCourant's modifications begin.

Files modified relative to that tag carry a
"Modified by the OpenCourant project" line in their header. Files
without that line are unchanged from upstream apart from a one-time
header normalization (see below).
